Hi, I'm Gabriel Biener

I am an optical scientist and engineer with 20+ years of experience developing advanced optical systems, microscopy, spectroscopy, and computational imaging technologies. My research combines optical instrumentation, biomedical imaging, image reconstruction, and AI/ML to address challenging problems in science and medicine. I have developed custom and handheld microscopy systems, quantitative imaging methods for protein-protein interactions in living cells, laser and spectroscopy-based instruments, and computational approaches for extracting meaningful information from complex images. I am the author of 65+ peer-reviewed publications and an inventor on three U.S. patents.
